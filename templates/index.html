GotFish?

GotFish is a phishing detection analyzer built for identifying malicious links and emails that the naked eye might miss which threat actors often abuse this to hide their infrastructure powered by DNSTwist

  • DNSTwist Database: Pre-computed 2000+ typo variants per brand
  • View Character Breakdown in Unicode: Character spell out mode to show the analyst Unicode code points for characters
  • Homograph Attacks: Detects lookalike characters (е vs e, о vs o)
  • Typosquatting: Identifies domains similar to trusted brands
  • Suspicious TLDs: Flags commonly-abused domain extensions
  • Punycode Domain Handling: Detects domains starting with xn--, which can hide non-ASCII characters inside URLs
  • Risk Scoring: Calculates cumulative risk in percentage

Risk Levels

Critical (70+) - Likely phishing, avoid
High (40-69) - Multiple red flags detected
Moderate (20-39) - Some suspicious patterns
Low (10-19) - Minor concerns
Safe (0-9) - No threats detected
