The Brief

What the machine checks

Read the domain before the domain reads you.

Every input is spelled out character by character, split into scheme, subdomain, and root domain, then compared against a list of brands that phishers love to imitate. Punycode is decoded. Unicode lookalikes are named. Registered look-alike domains are enumerated through the public dnstwist.it service.

GotFish deliberately refuses to say "safe" or "malicious." A tool that renders verdicts trains you to stop thinking. This one lays out the evidence and leaves the thinking intact.

What you check

  • Confusable characters — Cyrillic о is not Latin o.
  • The root domain — is it actually operated by the brand?
  • Unrelated TLDs — why would PayPal write from a .tk?
  • Display names — does the sender name match the sending domain?
  • Subdomain stuffing — brands parked left of the real registrant.
